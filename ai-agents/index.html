Example

A new enquiry comes in while you are on a job. The system asks what the customer needs and saves the details. It answers the common questions. It offers a time to talk. Before the call, an AI assistant can prepare a summary for your review. Afterwards, the next steps are recorded, with reminders or approved messages based on your process.

FAQs

Do I need to know what I want to automate?

No. Start with what keeps taking up your time. We will help you work out whether AI, a simpler automation, or a change to the process is the right fit.

Can you work with the tools we already use?

That is where we start. We check what your existing tools can connect to and explain any limitations before agreeing on the build.
